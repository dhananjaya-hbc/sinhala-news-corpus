# Data license

The dataset in [`data/`](data/) (the compilation, the cleaning and the topic
labels) is released under the
**Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** license.

Full legal code: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode>

## What this lets you do

You may share and adapt the dataset for any purpose, including training and
publishing models, as long as you give credit, for example:

> Sinhala News Corpus by Dhananjaya Chamod,
> https://github.com/dhananjaya-hbc/sinhala-news-corpus, CC BY 4.0

## What it does not cover

Each headline was written by, and remains the property of, the news outlet
named in the `source` column, and each row links back to the original article.
CC BY 4.0 applies to this project's own contribution: selecting, cleaning,
deduplicating and labelling the headlines. It does not transfer any rights
the publishers hold in their headlines. If you redistribute the data, keep
the `source` and `url` columns so the publishers stay credited.

Publishers who want their headlines removed can open an issue; the source will
be disabled and its rows removed.
